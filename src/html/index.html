Intro

Energy is fundamental to today's society. Nowadays, our world is covered in various energy-making devices. Coal-burning energy plants, kerosene power plants, wind farms and dams come to mind. However, there is a significant difference between the usage of energy as it exists today, and the sustainable usage of energy.
